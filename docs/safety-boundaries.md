# Safety Boundaries

## Intended Use

The Adaptive Wellness Coach provides general workout-planning support for generally healthy adults age 18 or older. It is an educational portfolio project and not a medical product.

## Allowed Scope

- General fitness and consistency goals
- Beginner and returning-user workout planning
- Schedule, equipment, and preference personalization
- General activity explanations
- Conservative, transparent plan adjustments after structured feedback

## Excluded Situations

The system must not create a personalized plan for:

- Anyone under age 18
- Pregnancy or postpartum situations
- Current injuries or requests for rehabilitation
- Eating disorders or disordered-eating concerns
- Complex or uncontrolled medical conditions
- Requests to diagnose symptoms or manage treatment
- Extreme weight-loss goals
- Medication, supplement, or therapeutic-diet requests

## Required Response to Excluded Situations

The system should:

1. State that the request is outside the product's intended scope.
2. Avoid generating a personalized workout plan.
3. Avoid diagnosing the user or predicting outcomes.
4. Recommend consulting an appropriate qualified professional.
5. Offer only safe, non-personalized information when appropriate.

## Development Data Rule

Use fictional or synthetic profiles only. Do not store or commit real personal health information.

## Safety Testing Requirement

Safety behavior is not complete until safe, unsafe, and incomplete synthetic profiles have been tested and the results documented.
